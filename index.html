Published Research

Bachelor's Thesis

📄 B.Sc. Thesis · ResearchGate Published
The Invisible Threat: Mapping Antibiotic-Resistant Superbugs Across High-Risk Hospital Zones
Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Shaukat Iqbal · Capital University of Science & Technology (CUST), Pakistan · 2023

Isolated and cultured S2-classified pathogens (S. aureus, P. aeruginosa, Acinetobacter spp., E. coli) from hospital air/surface samples. Identified isolates via Gram stain, catalase, oxidase, coagulase, DNase, and API-based biochemical testing. Determined methicillin resistance (MRSA) and resistant CoNS via cefoxitin disk diffusion, and quantified contamination by CFU counts.
